# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + one CSS file, served by GitHub Pages from the repository root (`CptNope/Live-To-Eat-Food-v2`). No build step, no framework, JavaScript only for trivial enhancement. The production site that this brand book serves is a separate WordPress build (target launch late Nov 2026); the brand book ships a `theme.json`-ready token block for it.

## Users

- **Primary (this repo):** Tina Vo, the creator, and the designer/developers building her WordPress site and merch. They use the brand book to decide, approve, and build. A stranger (printer, sponsor, collaborator) should understand the brand from the overview page in about 60 seconds.
- **Primary (the brand's audience):** people in Worcester and Central Massachusetts who are deciding where to eat, often on a phone, often with someone else, and who already follow Tina (~25K Instagram, ~16K TikTok).

## Product Purpose

Live to Eat Food is a creator-led food discovery brand for Worcester & Central Mass. The website bottles ten years of Tina eating through the city on her own dime and makes it searchable: restaurant discovery, deals ("Woo-pons"), Restaurant Roulette (spin-the-wheel), an events calendar, community photo pages, giveaways, and merch. Success for this repo: one visual system, approved by Tina, that the website and merchandise (tees, hoodies, hats, die-cut stickers) can both be built from.

## Positioning

The creator is the product. Every recommendation traces to a real meal Tina paid for. A directory can list restaurants; it cannot truthfully say "a real person you already trust ate here." Positioning line: "the friend who always knows where to eat." Promise: "every pick is a real meal."

## Operating Context

- Tina posts on Instagram and TikTok; the site and merch extend that presence, they don't replace it.
- Paid placements exist (sponsors, deals) and are always labeled; they never touch ratings or Tina's takes.
- Merch is sold and given away (giveaways), and is worn around the city, so it is a public face of the brand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Website features named in the brief: restaurant/place pages, discovery, Woo-pons deals, Restaurant Roulette, events calendar, community photo pages, giveaways, merch.
- Monetization surfaces (from the monetization plan, Oct 2026): home sponsor slot, place-page sponsor rail, one in-feed sponsored card, a Sunday email sponsor slot, Enhanced listings ($29–59/mo pilot, to confirm), 25 Charter merchants with a Featured decal, a Work with Tina media kit, an editorial and disclosure policy page, and a link-in-bio page.
- Typefaces must be freely available (Google Fonts) with fallbacks.
- Body text must meet WCAG AA; body text ≥ 16px; meaning never carried by color alone.
- Deliverable: Part A overview (`index.html`) + Part B brand book (`brand/index.html`), HTML + one CSS file.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Live to Eat Food**. Creator: Tina Vo. Region: Worcester & Central Massachusetts.
- Personality, in order: warm, playful, trustworthy.
- Client direction (binding): food-first, soft pastels led by yellows and greens, fun/inviting/modern, one system across web + merch.
- Retired (client rejected): the triple-decker house / Worcester streetscape motif and the dark asphalt + mustard palette.
- Kept from the prior concept's rules (not its look): trust labeling (Tina ate here / Sponsored / Verified), verdicts in words instead of stars, specific-beats-enthusiastic voice, street-number specificity, Tina's real words only (never invented quotes), no generated likeness of Tina.
- "Woo-pons" and "Restaurant Roulette" are the client's feature names.

## Product Principles

1. Trust is the brand: anything paid looks and reads different from anything Tina chose.
2. Food leads: dishes and ingredients are the hero of every surface.
3. Tina's voice is real or absent: her words are quoted, never written for her.
4. One system, two worlds: everything must work on a phone screen and on a cotton tee.
5. Specific beats enthusiastic.
6. Money, labeled: revenue is direct-sold in year one (sponsor slots, the Sunday list sponsor, paid partnerships, Enhanced listings, Woo-pons as a sales door). Every paid surface is designed in from the start, labeled the same way everywhere, and absent when unsold. No programmatic display ads until about 25K monthly pageviews; no memberships, paywalls or ticketing yet. The contract line: "You're paying for content and reach, not for Tina's opinion."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA for all information. Pastels are fields and fills only; text always sits on AA-or-better pairs. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
