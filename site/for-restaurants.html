Invite Tina

Restaurants can invite her. They can’t book a review. If she comes, she says what she thinks, the good and the honest. If you cover the meal, the visit is labeled Hosted, in words, above her take, and it doesn’t get the stamp or a window decal.

Say whether you’d cover the meal. If you do, the visit is labeled Hosted.

Sponsored

Advertise, labeled

Sponsored placements pay for the site. They follow these rules, every time:

  1. Every paid placement carries the hatched Sponsored label.
  2. Never larger than the Tina pick beside it, and never inside her take, her handwriting or the Sunday list.
  3. Sponsor photos are labeled as the sponsor’s.
  4. Paying never changes what Tina says or where a place ranks.
  5. Want Tina to make a post for you? That’s a paid partnership: labeled with your name, no verdict, never ranked.
