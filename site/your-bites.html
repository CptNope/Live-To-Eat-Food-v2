How bites work

This page is yours. Tina’s stamp and handwriting never appear here, so nobody mistakes a reader’s plate for her pick.

  • Your handle goes on every photo we share.
  • We ask before we post, every time.
  • Bites, not people: food first, faces only if you want.
  • Say where you went and what made you try it, so the next person can go.
