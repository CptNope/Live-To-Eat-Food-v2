What the labels mean

Tina ate here · Oct

She visited, paid, ate and wrote the take. The month is when she went.

Hosted

The restaurant invited Tina and covered the meal. Her take and verdicts are still her own, and a line above them says who paid. Hosted visits never get the stamp.

Paid partnership with [brand]

A brand paid Tina to make a post. It’s an ad in her voice: no verdict, never ranked, never on Roulette or in guides.

Verified business

The owner confirmed the hours and details. It isn’t an endorsement.

Tina hasn’t been yet

Listed so the map is complete. No take, no verdict, no handwriting.

Sponsored

A paid placement. Never bigger than the picks around it, never in Tina’s hand.

Referral link

We may earn a fee if you book. Never inside Tina’s take.

I’d get this againGood, not the one

Verdicts replace stars. Five phrases, only from places she ate at.
