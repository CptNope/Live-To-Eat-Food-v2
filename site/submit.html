Send something in

Every form on the site in one place: what each one is for, what it costs, and what happens after you press send.

LTEF · Send something in

What happens next

  1. 1Tina reads it. She goes through submissions every night or every other night, so expect a reply within about two days.
  2. 2She checks it’s real, and calls or emails if something needs a look. She can say no to anything, paid or not.
  3. 3Paid things are confirmed first. We email the price and how to pay before anything is charged.
  4. 4It goes up, labeled for what it is. Paying never changes what Tina says about a place.
