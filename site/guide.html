New since 2025, and Tina's already been

Three places that opened recently, in the order they opened: candlepin and flatbread in the Canal District, stir-fry off Route 9, and the Mongolian BBQ that got Tina to Leominster.

3 openings, May 2025 to early 2026

LTEF · Guide · New since 2025

  1. 1
    Tina ate here · May 2025

    Stop 1: American Flatbread Co

    85 Green St, Canal District · Opened May 10, 2025

    “Please don't crowd the place cause I wanna go back.”

    Wood-fired flatbread upstairs and ten candlepin lanes downstairs, inside The Cove. It brought public candlepin back to Worcester for the first time since Colonial Bowling closed in 2020.

    Tina ate here · Jul 2025

    Stop 2: honeygrow

    193 Boston Turnpike, Shrewsbury · Opened July 7, 2025

    “Ca-noodling around with some honeygrow. What's your order?”

    Made-to-order stir-fry and salads at Lakeway Commons, right off Route 9. Pick a protein and a base; the honeybars are the dessert.
