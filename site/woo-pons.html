Local deals.

Woo-pons are local deals around Worcester, sent in by the businesses themselves. Some are at places Tina loves, some at places she hasn’t tried yet, and every ticket says which. Show it at the counter. Businesses pay to list them, and that never buys a pick. A deal a business pays extra to promote is marked Sponsored.

Using a Woo-pon

  1. 1Tap Show at the counter. The ticket fills the screen, big enough for staff to read.
  2. 2Show it before you pay. Staff read the code and the date at the counter.
  3. 3Staff mark it used. They confirm first, and a slip can be undone. The fine print says how often you can use it.

Run a Woo-pon

Your first two weeks of Woo-pons are free, once. After that it’s $10 a week (pricing to confirm). Tell us the deal, the dates and the fine print. Every Woo-pon is reviewed before it goes live, and we can turn any down.

Offer a Woo-pon
  1. 1Send the offer and the end date.
  2. 2We check the place and the deal are real.
  3. 3It goes live as a ticket people show at the counter.
