How Tina picks

She goes, she eats, she posts

Every “Tina ate here” label means she was there. Places she hasn’t been are listed and clearly marked.

Honest, even about chains

An average buffet gets called an average buffet, even when she still goes sometimes. That’s the tone.

Nobody buys her opinion

Restaurants can invite her. They can’t book a review, and ads are always labeled.
