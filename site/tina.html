A note from Tina

I am so grateful you’re here! Welcome to Live to Eat Food (LTEF). It all goes back to a quote I once heard: “Do you eat to live, or do you live to eat?”

Clearly, I live to eat — and since you’re here, I’m guessing you do too.

My love for food started at home, where food was the ultimate love language. There weren’t many “I love yous” — instead, there were home-cooked meals, favorite dishes, and delicious food shared together.

That same feeling is at the heart of Live to Eat Food. What started as my love for eating has grown into a community built around sharing experiences, discovering new places, supporting small businesses, and giving people like you and me another reason to get out of the house and enjoy life.

Thank you for being part of this journey with me. 🫶🏼

Love,
Tina

How Tina picks

She goes, she eats, she posts

Every “Tina ate here” label means she was there. Places she hasn’t been are listed and clearly marked.

Honest, even about chains

An average buffet gets called an average buffet, even when she still goes sometimes. That’s the tone.

Nobody buys her opinion

Restaurants can invite her. They can’t book a review, and ads are always labeled.
