Editorial and disclosure policy

How Live to Eat Food keeps what Tina chose apart from what someone paid for. If you can’t tell which one you’re looking at in two seconds, we got it wrong: tell us.

Last updated [date] · A proposal for Tina to confirm before launch

LTEF · Editorial policy

Tina pays for her meals

Tina ate here · Oct 2026

Unless a page says otherwise, Tina found the place, paid for the meal and wrote the take. That’s what the Tina ate here label and the stamp mean, always with the month she went.

Hosted visits

Hosted

Sometimes a restaurant invites Tina and covers the meal. The page says Hosted, in words, above her take, and names who paid. Her verdicts are still hers. Hosted visits never get the stamp or a window decal.

Paid partnerships

Paid partnership

When a brand pays Tina to make a post or video, it’s an ad in her voice. It’s labeled with the brand’s name here and on the platform it runs on, has no verdict, and never enters rankings, guides, Roulette or the Monthly Crumbs picks.

Sponsored placements

Sponsored

Paid spots on the site and in Monthly Crumbs are written in the sponsor’s voice and carry the hatched Sponsored label. They’re never bigger than the picks around them, never in Tina’s hand, and never numbered or ranked. When nobody has bought a spot, it isn’t shown at all.

We sell these directly. There are no ad networks, pop-ups or autoplay video on Live to Eat Food.

Enhanced listings and Charter merchants

Enhanced listingCharter merchant

Restaurants can pay to add their own photos, menu, story and booking links to their page. That content is labeled as theirs and sits after Tina’s take. It doesn’t change her take, the order of any list, or whether a place is in a guide. Basic listings are free forever.

At launch, 25 Charter merchants get Enhanced free for 90 days in exchange for a Featured decal. The decal says “Paid listing” on it.

Woo-pons

Woo-pon

Woo-pons are paid listings: a business’s first two weeks are free, then it pays a weekly fee. Each ticket still says whether Tina has eaten there (“Tina ate here” or “Tina hasn’t been yet”), and paying never changes her take. A business that pays extra to promote a deal gets the Sponsored label and its own row. Online codes from brands Tina works with carry “Paid partnership with [brand]”.

Referral links

Referral link

Some booking links pay us a fee. They say “We may earn a fee if you book”, and they never sit inside Tina’s take.

What money can’t buy

  • Tina’s take, a verdict or a word of her handwriting
  • The Tina ate here stamp or label
  • A place in a guide, on Restaurant Roulette or in the Monthly Crumbs picks
  • Where you appear in Explore or any list
  • Seeing a post before it goes up, or a review in advance
  • Removing or softening an honest review

“You’re paying for content and reach, not for Tina’s opinion.”

Two doors

Suggestions for Tina go through Suggest a spot, even from owners, and nothing about them is for sale. Anything commercial goes through Work with Tina. The two never share a form or an inbox.

Everything is reviewed, and we can say no

Nothing anyone sends in goes up on its own. Listings, menus, Woo-pons, events, specials, job posts, partner listings and photos all wait until Tina approves them.

We can turn down any business, deal or event, including businesses that don’t welcome everyone or treat guests or staff badly. Paying doesn’t change that, and a business we’ve said no to can’t buy its way onto the site.

Corrections

Facts (hours, prices, addresses) are fixed as soon as we hear, and the page shows when they were last checked. Opinions are Tina’s and aren’t edited for anyone.
