Food help

Free food, meals and help around Worcester, and how to get SNAP (food stamps). No ads and no sign-up. If you need food today, start with the hotline or the food bank’s map.

Call ahead before you go. Hours change, some places need an appointment or an ID, and holidays close doors. Every number below goes straight to the place.

SNAP (food stamps)

SNAP puts money for groceries on an EBT card each month. In Massachusetts it’s run by the Department of Transitional Assistance (DTA). Project Bread’s hotline above can also help you apply.

Apply online

DTA Connect, on the web or the app. Start with the screener to see if you qualify.

Apply on DTA Connect (opens a new tab)Check if you qualify (opens a new tab)

Apply by phone

877-382-2363

The DTA Assistance Line. Staff Monday to Friday, 8:15am–4:45pm; press 7 to apply. Interpreters in over 100 languages.

Apply in person

DTA Worcester office · 50 Southwest Cutoff

508-767-3100

Monday to Friday, 8am–5pm. Wheelchair accessible, on WRTA bus routes, with parking.

Using SNAP on fresh produce

With HIP (the Healthy Incentives Program), SNAP households get $40 to $80 a month back on fruit and vegetables bought from farmers markets and farm stands that take it. This winter, the REC Mobile Market (opens a new tab) keeps stopping around Worcester, Tuesday to Thursday, through Dec 17. Find other HIP sellers on DTA Finder (opens a new tab).

Self-service DTA kiosks: MassHire Worcester, 340 Main St, Suite 400 (weekdays 8:45am–4:30pm; DTA staff there Tuesdays and Thursdays) and Ascentria, 11 Shattuck St (weekdays 9am–5pm).

Giving food?

Yes: produce, breads and pastries, unopened frozen food and meals, pantry staples like canned goods, flour and sugar, and sealed, labeled dairy and deli meats.

Homemade: single portions, not trays, each labeled with the date and the ingredients. Clear packaging helps.

No: raw meat, anything expired, moldy or already opened.

Fridges are cleaned and stocked regularly by volunteers, but anyone can drop off food or stop by to clean. The fridges are open 24/7. Check Woo Fridge’s Instagram for the latest.
