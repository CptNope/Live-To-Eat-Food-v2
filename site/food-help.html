Food help

Free food, meals and help around Worcester. No ads, no sponsors, no sign-up. If you need food today, start with the hotline or the food bank’s map.

LTEF · Food help

Start here

Call or text Project Bread

1-800-645-8333

The FoodSource Hotline. Monday to Friday 8am–7pm, Saturday 10am–2pm, in 180 languages. Help with SNAP, school and summer meals, pantries and hot meals.

projectbread.org

Find a pantry near you

The Worcester County Food Bank supplies pantries and meal programs across the county. Its map finds the closest ones by town or address.

Giving food?

Yes: produce, breads and pastries, unopened frozen food and meals, pantry staples like canned goods, flour and sugar, and sealed, labeled dairy and deli meats.

Homemade: single portions, not trays, each labeled with the date and the ingredients. Clear packaging helps.

No: raw meat, anything expired, moldy or already opened.

Fridges are cleaned and stocked by volunteers. Check Woo Fridge’s map or Instagram for the latest before you go.
