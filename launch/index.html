LTEF · Launch questions · Build and launch

Already decided

Settled with Tina in October and at the Nov 2 meeting, and live across the site, the brand book and the design files.

  • Outfit 700 for headings, place names, deal amounts and the wordmark: the bubble letters are retired
  • Sushi roll + bagel as the wordmark’s o’s
  • The plate as the “Tina ate here” stamp
  • Two verdicts: Favorite (star) and Don’t miss (target)
  • A barcode on the Woo-pons ticket
  • Woo-pons say “local deals”, are coloured by kind and can be at any business
  • Woo-pons: 2 weeks free once, then $10 a week; VIP $100, in Tina’s monthly video, up to 10 a month, with a flyer uploaded for the video
  • Woo Fridges: five, with St. Matthew’s and the new Providence St fridge (checked with Woo Fridge)
  • The Woo-pons page explains using one, then running one, right after the deals
  • Premium event listings $50 a month (listing stays free); job posts $100 a month
  • Ad spots on the site stay; an unsold one says “Ad space open” and invites businesses to advertise
  • “Paid ad” instead of “Sponsored”
  • No merch
  • Giveaways are for any business, not just restaurants
  • Events is Out & About Worcester+
  • Roulette spins only Tina’s places or every restaurant, and her places link to her video
  • Your bites takes any place people tried because of Tina
  • Photos with Tina: only photos she posed for
  • Every submission waits for Tina, and she can turn anyone down
  • Send something in: every form, its price and what happens next
  • Food help: no ads; call ahead; SNAP and phone numbers
  • Business cards with a QR code to For restaurants
  • Soft launch the first week of November
  • Site credit to Jeremy Anderson Tech, with a minimal partner disclosure
  • Real partner links: Lensmart (LTEF), LCI Paper, her Amazon storefront
  • Her letter, her photos and her press on the Tina page

The agreement between Tina and Jeremy is handled separately and isn’t tracked here.
