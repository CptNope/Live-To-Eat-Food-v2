## Scope
Brand book site for Live to Eat Food: `index.html` (overview, Persuade: a stranger gets the brand in 60s), `brand/index.html` (full book, Read), `merch/index.html` (merch lookbook, Persuade). One CSS file.

## Audience & job
Tina Vo and her builders/printers decide, approve and build from it. Action: open the brand book; copy tokens.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The brand is a sticker sheet. Every mark, label and food illustration is a die-cut sticker you could peel off and stick on a laptop, tee or takeout bag; the site refuses the creator-brand default of a pastel hero over a row of equal cards.
OWN-WORLD: Butter, lemon, pistachio and matcha backing sheets with a faint kiss-cut liner; white vinyl die-cut borders, basil-green 2.5px ink outlines, soft lifted shadow, hairline kiss-cut rings; Outfit 700 display, Figtree reading, Bricolage Grotesque labels, Shantell Sans for Tina's hand in tomato.
STORY: Visitor sees food-first, fun, trustworthy; understands the yolk wordmark, the "Tina ate here" stamp and the pastel rules; opens the book or copies tokens.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed butter sticker sheet with printer's edge strip (sheet label, cut marks). Giant die-cut wordmark sticker left-centre, ~10 food stickers and the round trust stamp scattered at slight rotations, one-paragraph intro set on the sheet, primary button "Open the brand book" as a pill sticker, secondary "See the merch".
FORM: Sticker Sheet, candidate 2 of 7 on my ordered list (user chose Impeccable's pick over assigned #6 Shared Table); seed key 5c4c817d.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
